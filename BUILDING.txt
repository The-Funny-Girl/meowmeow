Building kirkware
===============

Requirements
- 64-bit Windows with PowerShell 5.1 or newer
- LLVM-MinGW (clang++, windres, llvm-objdump, llvm-readobj, llvm-nm and llvm-strip)

From this folder, run:

  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\BUILD.ps1 -Toolchain "C:\path\to\llvm-mingw"

The finished executable is written to:

  build\kirkware.exe

The build script checks the bundled assets, imports and final executable
before printing its SHA-256 hash. If a tool cannot be found, point
-Toolchain at either the LLVM-MinGW root folder or its bin folder.
